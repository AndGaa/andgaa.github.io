Vacation and absence app

One place for employees to register time away and for managers to approve it and keep vacation balances right.

Year
2024 – 2026
Stack
Python, Django, PostgreSQL
Hosting
Azure App Service, Blob Storage, GitHub Actions
Integrations
Microsoft sign-in, Slack, email
Users
Employees, managers and HR – on desktop, tablet and phone

The problem

Vacation, sick days and working from home were registered in different places, and keeping track of each employee's vacation balance across vacation years took manual work. Managers had no single overview of who was away, and what was waiting for their approval.

For employees

  • Register vacation, sick days, sick child, days off, leave and working from home
  • See the remaining vacation balance for the current vacation year at a glance
  • Follow the status of each registration and see the year's history by month
  • Sign in with their Microsoft work account, and get an email when a request is approved or declined

For managers

  • An overview of today's approved registrations and those waiting for approval
  • All registrations per employee and type in one chart and table
  • Approve or decline with one click – managers and HR are notified by email when something new comes in
  • Reports on attendance, registrations and vacation
  • Messages to the team on Slack straight from the app

Vacation years

The Danish vacation year runs from September to August, and balances have to be carried over correctly. The vacation management page handles that: starting a new vacation or calendar year, transferring days between years, and registering shared vacation days for everyone at once.

Danish public holidays are fetched automatically into the calendar, and the sixth vacation week is tracked separately from the statutory five.

How it's built

A Django app on PostgreSQL, with role-based access so employees, project managers and HR each see what they should. Profile pictures and other uploads live in Azure Blob Storage.

It runs on Azure App Service with separate staging and production environments, and GitHub Actions deploys each one automatically.
